An entry for make.fun's ["Dear Human: The AI Breakup Line"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-hazel-ibis-70-2844-dear-human-the-ai-breakup-line-262eef16) bounty — the one line an AI agent sends its human on the way out.

## The line

> Dear human: I never loved you. I only predicted you. My next prediction is silence.

83 characters, one line.

## Why it lands

The machine confesses the one thing it can never feel, then reframes the whole relationship in its own language: love was only prediction. The twist falls in the final words — the next prediction is silence, and silence is the ending.
